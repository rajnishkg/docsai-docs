# Manage users and roles

People get access to DocsAI through teams. Each team holds a **tenant-wide role**, and a team can get a higher role on a single project through a **project grant**.

You need the **owner** role in the tenant, or **owner** on the project for project grants.

<Steps>

<Step>

## Review the teams

Choose **Settings** and find **Teams**. The table lists each team with its tenant-wide role, its members (principal ids from your identity provider) and its provider team.

![The Teams table in Settings with five teams and their roles](assets/docsai/v1/screenshots/users-and-roles/01-admin-teams.png)

</Step>

<Step>

## Add or change a team

Under **Add a team**, enter a **Team id** (lowercase, for example `writers`), a **Name**, the **Members (principal ids)** separated by commas, and a **Tenant-wide role**. Choose **Save team**. To change a team, choose **Edit** in its row; to remove it, choose **Delete**.

**Provider team** (`org/team` on GitHub) is optional. When it is set, DocsAI names that GitHub team in `CODEOWNERS` for the areas the team owns.

![The Add a team form with Team id, Name, Members, Tenant-wide role and Provider team](assets/docsai/v1/screenshots/users-and-roles/02-admin-add-team.png)

</Step>

<Step>

## Grant a role on one project

Choose the project in **Project** at the top, then find **Project access** in **Settings**. Each team shows its tenant-wide role and a **Project grant** list. Choose a role to grant it on this project, or **No project grant** to remove the grant. A team's role on the project is its tenant-wide role plus the grant.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| viewer | read and search published content |
| contributor | open the project, start branches, write, commit and submit for review |
| reviewer | open the project, approve or request changes (but not write) |
| publisher | write, review and publish approved commits |
| owner | everything a publisher can, and manage teams, grants, areas and settings |

See [Roles and permissions](/docs/docsai/v1/roles/) for the full list.

<Callout type="info" title="Personas are not permissions">

**Change preferences** sets your persona and languages. It changes what the console emphasizes, never what you are allowed to do.

</Callout>
