# Review and approve

Reviewers decide on the exact commit an author submitted. You need the **reviewer** role on the project, and for files in an ownership area, membership of the owning team.

<Steps>

<Step>

## Find the submission

Open the bell in the console header, or choose **Content**, the project, and the author's branch in **Working branch**. **Review status** shows *submitted*.

</Step>

<Step>

## Read the change

Read **Git diff from cloned source**, and open changed pages with **Preview** to see them as readers will. Select text in the preview, or choose **Comment** beside a heading, to start a discussion on a line.

</Step>

<Step>

## Decide

Type an optional **Commit review comment**, then choose **Approve exact commit** or **Request commit changes**. An approval answers *Exact commit approved.*

</Step>

</Steps>

An approval belongs to that commit only. If the author commits again, the approval does not carry over.

## Publish

When the reviewer also has the **publish** permission, approving starts the reader release. Otherwise the change waits for a publisher, who opens the branch and chooses **Publish approved commit to current guides**. See [Publish the reader site](/docs/docsai/v1/publish-the-reader-site/).
